Frontend developer who cares about the details

I'm Uday, a frontend developer based in India. I enjoy the part of the job where a rough idea slowly becomes something real on screen — the spacing, the motion, the moment an interaction finally feels right.

My work sits between design and engineering. I care about clean, readable code as much as I care about how a button feels when you hover it. Every project I take on gets built responsive, accessible and fast by default — not as an afterthought.

When I'm not building, I'm usually exploring new tools, reading about design systems, or rebuilding something I made last year because I know I can do it better now.
